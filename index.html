🛡️ The Emergency Fund Rule

Aim to keep 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses (rent + food + utilities) in a high-interest liquid account. VOX keeps this amount ring-fenced so your Safe-to-Spend never leaves you vulnerable.

📊 The 50 / 30 / 20 Budget Principle

Allocate 50% of take-home pay to Needs (rent, groceries, basic bills), 30% to Wants (dining, streaming, entertainment), and 20% to Savings and Debt repayment.

⚡ Debt Avalanche vs Snowball

Avalanche: Pay minimum on all debts, attack highest interest debt first (mathematically optimal).
Snowball: Pay smallest balance first for psychological momentum.
